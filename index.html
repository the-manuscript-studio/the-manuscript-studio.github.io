About us

Research support built on clarity, rigour and integrity.

The Manuscript Studio was created for medical and dental students, postgraduate residents, clinicians and researchers who have valuable ideas but limited time to navigate the complex path from research question to publication.

We combine methodological expertise, statistical know-how and careful academic editing to make your research more structured, transparent and publishable. Whether you are starting your first project, completing a thesis or preparing a systematic review, we work alongside you and help you understand every step.

Our approach is collaborative: you stay the author and owner of your work, and we provide the guidance, analysis support and polish that help it reach its full potential.

Methodological rigour

Evidence-based frameworks and recognised reporting guidelines such as PRISMA, CONSORT and STROBE, applied to every project.

Clear communication

We explain the why behind every decision, so you can confidently present and defend your work before guides, examiners and reviewers.

Integrity & confidentiality

Your data and ideas are handled confidentially, and your ownership of your academic work always stays central.

Ask a Question
Who do you work with?
We support medical and dental undergraduates, postgraduate residents (MD, MS, MDS), clinicians, faculty members, PhD scholars and independent researchers, whether it's a first project or a complex meta-analysis.
Do you write papers and theses?
Yes. Share your topic, objectives, data and any guidelines from your institution or target journal, and our team will write your paper or thesis for you. We then refine the draft based on your feedback until you're completely satisfied.
How is pricing decided?
Every project is different. After understanding your scope, current progress, complexity and deadline, we share a clear, itemised quote before any work begins, with no hidden charges.
How long does a project take?
Timelines depend on the service. A consultation can be scheduled within days, while a full systematic review may take several weeks. We agree on realistic milestones with you upfront.
Is my data kept confidential?
Yes. Your research data, manuscripts and ideas are handled confidentially and used only for your project. We're happy to sign a confidentiality agreement on request.
Can you guarantee publication?
No ethical service can guarantee acceptance, since editorial decisions rest with journals and peer reviewers. What we do is maximise your manuscript's quality, clarity, compliance and fit with the right journal.
